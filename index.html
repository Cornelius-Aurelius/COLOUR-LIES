THE IDEA BEHIND THE GAME

COLOUR IS LOUD. ATTENTION IS POWER.

Colour Lies — Signal Override is a free browser-based reaction and pattern recognition arcade game originally created by Cornelius Aurelius. A word may appear in another colour; the player must follow the written mission instruction rather than a distracting visual cue. Later chapters introduce non-colour symbol recognition.

The game is designed for fun and awareness of conflicting visual information, not as a colour-blindness test, vision simulation, medical diagnosis or clinical cognitive assessment. Instructions and solutions are provided through words and shapes, so the correct answer never requires identifying an ink colour.
