# Colour Lies — Signal Override

A complete free web-based reaction and pattern recognition arcade game created by **Cornelius Aurelius**.

**Published URL:** https://cornelius-aurelius.github.io/COLOUR-LIES/
**Creator:** https://corneliusaurelius.com/

## Gameplay

- Campaign: thirty progressively challenging stages, five chapters, three shields, word/shape missions, scores and local-only unlocks.
- Endless: escalating score and personal best.
- Practice: no timer and unlimited mistakes.
- Goal: follow the written rule, choose the printed **word** (ignore misleading ink), or choose the displayed **shape** on certain stages. Answers are never colour-only.
- Inputs: mouse/touch, keyboard `1`–`4`, `F` focus hint, `Esc`/`P` pause, `Enter` next stage.
- Focus hint removes incorrect choices and costs 25 points. No account, payments, analytics or external dependencies.

## Accessibility

Strong colour contrast, labels and shapes rather than colour alone, high-contrast theme, focus rings, pause when tab is hidden, reduced-motion CSS, no forced automatic advance on a correct/wrong answer. Not a medical test, clinical assessment or colour vision simulation.
